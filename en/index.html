Lagvis – what to wear?

Get the answer every morning, layer by layer, based on the weather where you live. For children aged 1 to 6, whether the day is at preschool or at home.

  • Advice for the whole day, 08:00–16:00, based on the coldest hour.
  • Layer by layer: base layer, mid layer, outer layer, footwear, hat and mittens.
  • No account and no tracking. Your child's name and age stay on your phone.
